# Haila illustration brief

Use case: compositing.
Asset type: finished mobile app mascot illustration for the restaurant outing loading screen.
Input 1 is the existing Haila character: green watercolor cardamom pod with a golden scarf.
Input 2 is a screenshot; use ONLY the small cardamom character holding a folded map in the central cream mobile screen as a second character reference. Ignore all app chrome, text, logos and people elsewhere.
Merge the two Haila designs into ONE coherent charming mascot: keep the hand-painted watercolor texture, natural olive/sage cardamom ribs and ochre scarf from input 1; borrow the elegant slightly curling pointed top, expressive green eyes, tiny golden shoes and little folded cream map from the central mascot in input 2. Warm clever friendly expression, subtle smile, restrained eyes with soft highlights, not overly babyish. Small rounded leaf-like hands gently hold the map open as she finds a place for friends to eat. Make the shape unmistakably a cardamom pod, not an onion or pear. Slimmer and more graceful than input 1 but recognizably the same character. Forest green, muted olive, warm golden yellow, cream paper. Beautiful delicate watercolor edges and pigment texture, polished editorial storybook illustration.
Composition: single full-body character centered, including scarf, map and shoes, portrait silhouette occupying about 85% of the canvas height with modest clear margin. No surrounding scenery, no extra floating objects, no typography. Genuine transparent alpha background, not a baked white or cream rectangle, not a checkerboard. No watermark, no labels, no signature. Keep the scarf proportionate so the face and map remain legible when rendered 260 px tall.
This is a reusable production image asset, not a screen mockup.
